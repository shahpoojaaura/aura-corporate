Privacy Policy

How The Aura House handles your details

Effective 10 September 2026 · Ohm Global Wellness Private Limited, Mumbai, India

1. Who we are

The Aura House is operated by Ohm Global Wellness Private Limited (“the Company”, “we”), a company registered in India, based in Mumbai. GSTIN 27AAFCO0974P1ZT. You can reach us at contact@theaurahouse.co or on +91 93200 42432. This policy explains what we collect on this page, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

2. What we collect, and how

  • The enquiry form. Your name, company, phone number, email address, what you are looking to run, the time you sent it, and the fact that you ticked the consent box. Company is optional. We ask for nothing else, and we do not ask for anything sensitive.
  • Booking a discovery call. Bookings run through Google Calendar’s appointment scheduling. Google collects what it needs to make the booking under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, your email address and the time you chose.
  • WhatsApp. If you message us, WhatsApp (a Meta service) handles the message under its own terms. We see your number, your profile name and what you write.
  • Nothing automatically. This page runs no analytics, no advertising trackers and sets no cookies of its own. The service that hosts the page may keep standard server logs (your IP address, browser type, time of visit) to deliver it, under that service’s own policy. Typefaces are served from this site itself; nothing is requested from Google to draw the page.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

To reply to you, to understand what you want to run, to scope and send a proposal, and to keep a record of the conversation. We rely on your consent, given when you tick the box on the form or choose to message or book us. If a programme follows, we also rely on the contract between us. We do not use your details for advertising, and we do not sell or rent them to anyone.

4. Where it is kept, and who can see it

Enquiries are recorded in a Google Form and a Google Sheet inside our Google Workspace account. Google may store that data on servers outside India. Access is limited to the founder and the person handling enquiries. We share your details with no one else, except where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for 24 months from our last contact with you, then deleted. If you become a client, we keep what the engagement needs for as long as the engagement runs and for as long as Indian tax and company law requires business records to be kept. You can ask us to delete your enquiry earlier at any time, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it, withdraw your consent, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Email contact@theaurahouse.co with “Privacy request” in the subject. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Grievance contact

Grievance Officer: Pooja Shah, Founder. Email contact@theaurahouse.co, phone +91 93200 42432. Grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days.

8. Children

This page and our corporate programmes are for adults. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us details, tell us and we will delete them.

9. Security

Your details sit in named, password-protected accounts. Only the people named above can open them. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we take reasonable care.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. Where a change affects how we use details you have already given us, we will ask you again.
