Refunds and Cancellations

Fees, cancellations and refunds

Effective 10 September 2026 · Ohm Global Wellness Private Limited, Mumbai, India

1. No payments on this page

Nothing is bought or paid for on this page. A discovery call is free. Programmes are agreed in a written proposal and agreement before any fee is due.

2. What governs fees and refunds

Each proposal sets out its own fee, payment schedule, cancellation windows and refund terms, and those terms govern the engagement. Read them before signing. If a proposal is silent on a point, ask us before you sign and we will put the answer in writing.

3. Dates that move

Where a date has to move for reasons on our side, such as a venue or facilitator becoming unavailable, we offer a new date first. Where a date has to move for reasons on the client’s side, the proposal’s cancellation terms apply.
